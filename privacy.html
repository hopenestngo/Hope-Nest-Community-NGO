HOPENEST

Data Protection & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 7 June 2026 | Last Updated: 7 June 2026

1. Introduction

HOPENEST ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of our members, volunteers, partners, donors, beneficiaries, website visitors, and supporters.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose personal information obtained through our website, programs, events, and other activities.

By accessing our website, submitting forms, making donations, volunteering, partnering with us, or engaging with HOPENEST, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

2.1 Personal Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Physical address.
  • Date of birth.
  • Occupation.
  • Organization or company information.
  • Volunteer information.
  • Membership information.

2.2 Donation Information

When making a donation, we may collect:

  • Donor name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Donation amount.
  • Payment method.
  • Donation history.

Note: HOPENEST does not store full credit card or banking information. Payment transactions are processed securely through approved payment service providers.

2.3 Website Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type.
  • Device information.
  • Pages visited.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Website usage statistics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

3.1 Manage Memberships

  • Process membership applications.
  • Communicate updates and opportunities.
  • Maintain member records.

3.2 Manage Volunteers

  • Coordinate volunteer activities.
  • Provide training and support.
  • Contact volunteers about opportunities.

3.3 Manage Partnerships

  • Evaluate partnership requests.
  • Communicate regarding collaboration opportunities.
  • Manage ongoing partnerships.

3.4 Process Donations

  • Process contributions.
  • Issue receipts and acknowledgements.
  • Provide donor reports and updates.

3.5 Improve Our Services

  • Monitor website performance.
  • Improve programs and services.
  • Measure impact and effectiveness.

3.6 Legal Compliance

  • Meet legal, regulatory, and reporting obligations.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where applicable, HOPENEST processes personal information based on:

  • Your consent.
  • Performance of a contract or agreement.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate organizational interests that do not override your privacy rights.

5. Sharing of Information

HOPENEST does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We may share information only with:

5.1 Service Providers

Trusted third-party providers who assist with:

  • Website hosting.
  • Payment processing.
  • Email communication.
  • Data storage.

5.2 Legal Authorities

When required by law, court order, or regulatory obligation.

5.3 Program Partners

Only where necessary to implement projects and only with appropriate safeguards.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from:

  • Unauthorized access.
  • Alteration.
  • Disclosure.
  • Loss.
  • Misuse.

These measures include:

  • Secure servers.
  • Password-protected systems.
  • Access controls.
  • Staff confidentiality obligations.

However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Maintain organizational records.

When information is no longer needed, it will be securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

8.1 Access

Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

8.2 Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

8.3 Deletion

Request deletion of your information where legally permissible.

8.4 Restrict Processing

Request limitations on how your information is used.

8.5 Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for communications or processing based on consent.

8.6 Object

Object to certain types of data processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Enhance user experience

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although some website features may not function properly.

10. Children's Privacy

HOPENEST works with children and young people through its programs.

Where personal information relating to children is collected:

  • Appropriate consent will be obtained from parents, guardians, schools, or authorized representatives where required.
  • Additional safeguards will be implemented to protect children's information.

11. International Data Transfers

Where personal information is transferred outside your country, HOPENEST will take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect the information.

12. Photography & Media

During HOPENEST activities, events, and programs, photographs, videos, testimonials, and success stories may be collected for awareness, reporting, fundraising, and promotional purposes.

Where required:

  • Consent will be requested before publication.
  • Individuals may request removal of published content via email where appropriate.

13. Changes to this Policy

HOPENEST may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on our website together with the updated effective date. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
