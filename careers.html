Volunteer • Flexible (Part-Time / Full-Time) • Uganda

Volunteer Opportunity

Organisation: Hope Nest Community NGO

Position: Volunteer

Location: Uganda (Community-Based / Hybrid)

Position Type: Volunteer

Commitment: Flexible (Part-Time or Full-Time)

Application Deadline: 24 December 2026

About HOPENEST Organisation

HOPENEST Organisation is a non-profit organization committed to empowering women, girls, children with disabilities, youth, and vulnerable communities through education, health, environmental conservation, economic empowerment, digital innovation, and sustainable community development.

Role Summary

We are looking for passionate, dedicated, and community-minded volunteers to support our programmes and initiatives. Volunteers play an important role in helping us deliver impactful services, organize community activities, and improve the lives of the people we serve. Whether you are a student, graduate, or professional, volunteering with HOPENEST offers an opportunity to gain practical experience while making a meaningful difference.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support community outreach and awareness campaigns.
  • Assist in organizing workshops, trainings, and community events.
  • Support education and mentorship programmes.
  • Participate in environmental conservation activities.
  • Assist in women and youth empowerment initiatives.
  • Support programme implementation and field activities.
  • Help with office administration and documentation when required.
  • Collect community feedback and programme information.
  • Represent HOPENEST professionally in the communities we serve.

Who Can Apply?

  • Students, graduates, and professionals from all fields.
  • Individuals with a passion for community service and volunteerism.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Willingness to learn and contribute positively.
  • No previous volunteering experience is required.

Benefits of Volunteering

  • Hands-on experience in community development.
  • Professional mentorship and guidance.
  • Training and capacity-building opportunities.
  • Networking with development professionals and partners.
  • Volunteer Certificate upon successful completion.
  • Outstanding volunteers may be considered for future internship or employment opportunities.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit:

  • A cover letter explaining why you would like to volunteer with HOPENEST Organisation.
  • An updated Curriculum Vitae (CV).
  • Copies of academic certificates or other relevant supporting documents (where applicable).

Applications should be sent to hopenestcommunity.org2@gmail.com with the subject line:

Equal Opportunity

HOPENEST Organisation welcomes volunteers from all backgrounds regardless of gender, age, disability, ethnicity, religion, or social background. We believe diversity strengthens our mission and encourage everyone with a passion for community service to apply.

Digital & IT Support Specialist

Full-Time • Hybrid (On-site / Remote) • Uganda

Job Vacancy

Organisation: Hope Nest Community NGO

Position: Digital & IT Support Specialist

Location: Uganda (Hybrid – On-site / Remote)

Employment Type: Full-Time

Application Deadline: 24 December 2026

About HOPENEST Organisation

HOPENEST Organisation is a non-profit organization committed to improving the lives of vulnerable communities through sustainable development, education, health, environmental conservation, youth empowerment, disability inclusion, and digital innovation. We work with communities and partners to create lasting positive change through impactful programmes and technology-driven solutions.

Our Mission

At HOPENEST, we believe technology should empower communities and strengthen development programmes. We are committed to creating secure, innovative, and user-friendly digital systems that enhance communication, improve efficiency, and support our mission of empowering women, girls, youth, children with disabilities, and vulnerable communities.

The Impact You'll Make

As the Digital & IT Support Specialist, you will manage the organisation's digital infrastructure, website, ICT systems, cybersecurity, and technical support services. You will also help strengthen HOPENEST's digital presence and ensure reliable technology solutions that enable staff and programmes to operate efficiently.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and maintain the HOPENEST website.
  • Manage social media platforms and digital communication campaigns.
  • Create graphics, videos, and other digital promotional materials.
  • Provide ICT support for staff, office equipment, and digital systems.
  • Manage Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and cloud-based services.
  • Install, configure, and maintain computer hardware and software.
  • Implement cybersecurity best practices and protect organizational data.
  • Maintain ICT infrastructure including networks and internet connectivity.
  • Support technical requirements during trainings, workshops, and community events.
  • Recommend and implement technology solutions that improve organizational efficiency.

Qualifications

  • Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
  • Professional certifications such as CompTIA, Cisco, Microsoft, Google, AWS, or related certifications are an added advantage.

Experience

  • Minimum of two (2) years of ICT support or related professional experience.
  • Experience working with NGOs or development organizations is an added advantage.

Required Skills & Competencies

  • Website management and maintenance.
  • Social media and digital marketing skills.
  • Strong knowledge of networking and cybersecurity.
  • Excellent troubleshooting and technical support skills.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively.
  • Strong organizational and time management skills.

Why Join HOPENEST?

  • Work with a purpose-driven organization creating lasting community impact.
  • Professional development and continuous learning opportunities.
  • Supportive, collaborative, and inclusive work environment.
  • Hybrid working arrangements.
  • Competitive salary and benefits.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit:

  • A cover letter outlining their suitability for the position.
  • An updated Curriculum Vitae (CV) with at least three professional referees.
  • Copies of academic certificates and relevant professional certifications.
